# Research 02 — Existing systems (Pakistan + international) and a novelty check of our idea
Compiled: 2026-11-25

## A. Pakistan: how e-marking checks quality today
- CORRECTED (D-022): BISE PESHAWAR e-marking (Dawn, 12 Jan 2023). Three tiers: the first teacher
  marks, a second RANDOMLY reviews 20% of marked papers, and the head examiner intervenes
  where needed. Source: https://www.dawn.com/news/1731157 (earlier wrongly attributed to FBISE)
- FBISE chairman: "About 10pc papers are given to the head examiner", plus super examiners.
  A head examiner complained that the head examiner's role became limited under e-marking.
  Source: https://www.dawn.com/news/1772170
- FBISE uses rubric (step-wise) marking with e-sheets that have pre-allocated space per question.
  Source: https://apsacssectt.edu.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/FAQs-latest-Snr-School.pdf
- BISE Rawalpindi after the 2024 scandal: subject-teacher teams to check marking quality
  "at any time". Source: https://www.dawn.com/news/1872208
- Punjab: e-marking is gradual ("all exams to e-marking within the next two to three years").
  Source: https://pakobserver.net/lahore-board-announces-key-changes-to-exams-marking-system/
  -> For years, most subjects will still be marked by hand on paper, and rechecking will be on physical scripts.

PATTERN: quality checks in Pakistan = RANDOM samples (10–20%) of scripts.

## B. International benchmark
- Seeding: pre-marked scripts hidden in examiners' piles (Cambridge, IB, Ofqual).
- AQA research (Pinot de Moira): any QA system based on sampling "has little potential to
  influence marking reliability directly". It only identifies errant markers for retraining.
  Source: https://www.aqa.org.uk/about-us/our-research/research-library/identifying-errant-markers-quality-assurance-systems-in-an-e-marking-environment
  -> Sampling finds BAD EXAMINERS, not WRONG MARKS on a given student's script.

## C. NOVELTY CHECK — does "AI audits human marking" already exist? YES, as research/trials in the UK.
- Ofqual (2020) asked whether AI could act "as a second marker or as a way of monitoring marking",
  and whether it could spot "an erroneous mark from an otherwise good and consistent marker".
  Source: https://ofqual.blog.gov.uk/2020/01/09/exploring-the-potential-use-of-ai-in-marking
- AQA trialled AI "quality assurance" of human marking in summer 2024 (research only).
  Source: https://www.tes.com/magazine/news/general/gcses-2024-exam-board-trial-ai-summer-exams
- AQA + King's College London: an AI assistant to detect human marks that seem "erroneously
  low or high". Source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/ai-help-mark-student-exams-160000080.html
- Ofqual Jan 2026: AI "is promising for quality assurance and marker training" but not for marking itself.
  Source: https://ofqual.blog.gov.uk/2026/01/14/using-ai-in-marking-why-technical-capability-fairness-and-transparency-all-matter/
- AQA CEO, Nov 2026: AI could be used "pretty much tomorrow morning to mark markers". Only
  public confidence is holding it back.
  Source: https://schoolsweek.co.uk/only-public-confidence-stopping-us-from-using-ai-in-marking-says-aqa
- [NOT FOUND] Any Pakistani board using AI for marking quality assurance.

## What this means for us
- GOOD: the world's leading regulator and the largest English exam board say this is the right
  direction. That makes our idea credible, not science fiction.
- BAD: we can't claim the general concept "AI checks human markers" is ours. Saying so would
  be false, and a judge could find it in 2 minutes.
- So our originality has to be in WHAT we add for THIS context. See D-006.
