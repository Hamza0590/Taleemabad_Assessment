# 00 — KEY EVIDENCE (crucial, use these in the final write-up)
Saved: 2026-11-25. Each point has its source link. The full background is in 01-how-marking-works-and-figures.md.

## 1. Scale
- Punjab Matric (Class 10) 2026: 1,348,352 candidates appeared across 9 boards, 68.72% passed.
  Source: https://propakistani.pk/2026/08/06/punjab-10th-class-result-2026-least-intelligent-board-revealed/
- Punjab Class 9 2026: 1,395,575 appeared, ~45.98% passed. Rawalpindi was lowest at 37.56%.
  Source: https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/03/punjab-9th-class-result-2026-least-intelligent-board-revealed/
- [OUR ESTIMATE] ~2.74M matric candidates x ~8 theory papers = roughly 20M+ answer scripts per year
  (matric only, Punjab only). Always present this as an estimate.

## 2. How marking works: paid per paper, and paid MORE for marking MORE
- Punjab 2023 revision: matric examiners who check >50 papers get Rs 45/paper, <50 get Rs 35.
  Inter: Rs 50 (>50 papers) vs Rs 40 (<50).
  Source: https://propakistani.pk/2023/01/27/punjab-increases-matric-and-intermediate-paper-checking-fee/
- Quality check is a SAMPLE: at BISE Multan the head examiner rechecks 20% of each examiner's copies.
  Source: https://web.bisemultan.edu.pk/?p=6406
  (BISE Gujranwala practicals 2026: head examiner checks 17% of scripts:
   https://www.ilmkidunya.com/articles/bise-gujranwala-announces-remuneration-rates-for-practical-board-examiners-2026-3799)
  -> about 80% of scripts are never seen by a second person.

## 3. Documented failures
- BISE Rawalpindi SSC 2024: 435 marking staff punished for wrong marking (9 secrecy officials,
  66 head examiners, 210 sub-examiners, 119 assistants to head examiners, 22 special checkers,
  8 BISE representatives, 1 data entry operator). Penalties: warnings, salary cuts, 1–3 year bans.
  Source: https://www.dawn.com/news/1872208
- Case like the user's own: a Class 9 student found one question unmarked and other answers
  "ticked and under-credited without justification". The process is recounting, not rechecking.
  Source: https://tribune.com.pk/story/2584374/students-always-on-the-receiving-end (Dec 2025)
- Extreme case: a candidate got 2/75 originally and 61/75 after rechecking (RBISE, older report).
  Source: https://tribune.com.pk/story/665121/entertainment
- Until Oct 2025, rechecking only verified totals and unmarked questions, with "no room for
  reviewing marking accuracy".
  Source: https://www.techjuice.pk/?p=197961 ; https://propakistani.pk/2025/10/15/bise-lahore-to-replace-paper-rechecking-with-a-new-system/

## 4. What's changing, and the missing numbers
- Punjab boards are moving to e-marking (scanned scripts, examiner IDs, face verification,
  time limits) from 2026, piloting first.
  Sources: https://www.dawn.com/news/1960947 ; https://tribune.com.pk/story/2632845/punjab-boards-to-take-paper-checking-online
- Lahore board is replacing recounting with re-marking (announced Oct 2025, needs cabinet approval).
  Source: https://propakistani.pk/2025/10/15/bise-lahore-to-replace-paper-rechecking-with-a-new-system/
- MISSING: no board publishes how many rechecking requests it receives or how many marks change.
  Searched, nothing found. Story point: "the system cannot measure its own fairness."

## CAUTION 1 — the pay figures are from 2023
Say "as of the 2023 revision". Rates may have changed since.
