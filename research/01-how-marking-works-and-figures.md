# Research 01 — How board marking works in Punjab, the numbers, and what's changing
Compiled: 2026-11-25. Every figure has a source. Items marked [ESTIMATE] are our own arithmetic.
Items marked [NOT FOUND] were searched for but no public figure exists.

## 1. Scale (how many papers get marked)
- Class 10 (SSC-II) 2026, all 9 Punjab boards: 1,348,352 appeared, 926,569 passed (68.72%).
  Source: ProPakistani / Bloom Pakistan, from official gazettes, Aug 2026.
- Class 9 (SSC-I) 2026, all 9 Punjab boards: 1,395,575 appeared, 641,662 passed (~45.98%).
  Source: ProPakistani, Sep 2026.
- Rawalpindi board 2026: Class 10 61.31% (lowest in Punjab), Class 9 37.56% (lowest).
  HSSC-I 2025: 43.46%. Inter Part-II supplementary 2025: 31.9%.
  Sources: Dunya News, ProPakistani, Dawn Oct 2025, APP Dec 2025.

## 2. How manual marking works today
- Centralised marking centres. Roles: head examiner, sub-examiner, assistant to head examiner,
  super checker, coordinator (BISE Multan rate notice; FBISE centralised marking regulations).
- The head examiner prepares the marking scheme and guides sub-examiners (FBISE regulation 10).
- Pay is PER SCRIPT, and the rate goes UP if you mark more:
  - Punjab 2023 revision: matric Rs 45/paper if the examiner checks >50 papers, Rs 35 if <50;
    inter Rs 50 if >50, Rs 40 if <50. Assistants to head examiner get Rs 3.5/paper.
    (ProPakistani, Jan 2023)
  - BISE Multan: sub-examiner Rs 30/copy SSC; head examiner rechecks 20% of each examiner's copies.
    Super checker: Rs 700 for 200 copies/day. Coordinator: 150 copies/day. (BISE Multan notice)
  - BISE Gujranwala practicals 2026: Rs 18 (SSC) / Rs 21 (HSSC) per script; head examiner
    checks 17% of scripts. (ilmkidunya, Sep 2026)
  -> The incentive structure explicitly rewards volume. Quality control is a sample (17–20%).
- Comparison: CBSE India sets a norm of 20 answer books/day in main subjects over 8 hours,
  i.e. ~24 min each (CBSE marking-scheme instructions).

## 3. Documented failures
- Rechecking = recounting: until Oct 2025 it only verified mark totals and unmarked questions,
  "leaving no room for reviewing marking accuracy" (TechJuice / ProPakistani, Oct 2025).
  Board statutes call it recounting, not rechecking (Express Tribune op-ed, Dec 2025).
- BISE Rawalpindi SSC 2024: 435 marking staff punished, including 66 head examiners,
  210 sub-examiners, 119 assistants to head examiners and 22 special checkers. Penalties were
  warnings, salary cuts, and 1–3 year bans. (Dawn)
- Under-credited answers: a Class 9 student got a C in Urdu and A+ everywhere else. At the viewing
  she found an unmarked question and answers "ticked and under-credited without justification".
  The writer proposes flagging abrupt dips/surges in subject marks during tabulation.
  (Express Tribune, Dec 2025). This is the SAME pattern as the user's FSc Physics paper.
- Historic extremes: an RBISE computer science candidate went from 2/75 to 61/75 after
  rechecking (Express Tribune, older). An examiner gave 98 but the award list carried 88 (Dawn, older).
- Punjab Education Minister ordered action against examiners for incorrect marking. Complaints
  cover "incorrect marking, unchecked answers, calculation mistakes and inconsistencies between
  examiners" (Express Tribune, Oct 2026).
- [NOT FOUND] Province-wide numbers: rechecking applications per year, % of marks changed.
  A gap we can point out: boards don't publish marking-quality statistics.

## 4. What's changing (2025–2026)
- BISE Lahore: re-marking to replace recounting (announced Oct 2025, needs cabinet approval).
- Punjab: e-marking from 2026 (Dawn Dec 2025). Minister directed pilots for supplementary
  exams (Tribune Oct 2026). Lahore: on-screen marking from second annual exams, examiners mark
  from home (Eduvision / Bol, 2026). Computer science was the first pilot subject (ProPakistani Dec 2025).
- Features reported: scanned scripts, secure examiner IDs, face verification, time limits,
  central monitoring, real-time cameras on examiners (Tribune Oct 2026; Pak Observer).
- Elsewhere: FBISE has used e-marking since ~2018 with e-sheets (pre-allocated space per
  question) since 2021. BISE Peshawar e-marks English, Urdu, Physics, Chemistry. Karachi
  piloted Maths in 2025.
- Warning sign: an FBISE head examiner said the head examiner's checking role was LIMITED
  under e-marking (Dawn).

## 5. How quality is checked internationally (benchmark)
- Seeding: pre-marked scripts hidden in each examiner's allocation and compared with the
  principal examiner's marks (Cambridge, IB, Ofqual 2016 metrics report).
- AQA: a senior examiner checks the first 10 scripts, then 15 more halfway through.
- OSM removes clerical addition errors because the system sums the marks (Ofqual blog 2018).
-> All of these check EXAMINERS on SAMPLES. None checks every live student script.

## Takeaways for the story
1. The per-script pay that rises with volume rewards speed, and that's documented, not just anecdotal.
2. Quality control on judgement is a 17–20% sample (manual) or seeds (OSM). Most scripts are never second-read.
3. E-marking fixes addition errors and examiner identity, but nothing public says how it checks judgement.
4. Nobody publishes marking-error rates. The system can't measure its own fairness.
