# D-001 — Domain and how we source the problem
Date: 2026-11-24
Status: DECIDED

## Question
Should we pick a problem in education because Taleemabad is an edtech company, and how do we
avoid ending up with the same idea every other candidate gets from an AI tool?

## Options explored
1. Ask an AI tool for "the best edtech problem" and pick one.
2. Pick a problem outside education to look different.
3. Stay in education, but take the problem from my own lived experience and check it against
   real evidence (news, board data, people I talk to).

## Decision
Option 3.

## Why
- Option 1 gives the same handful of ideas everyone gets: an Urdu AI tutor, a lesson-plan
  generator, offline learning, auto-grading, personalized learning paths. Taleemabad already
  works in several of these areas, so pitching them back would show nothing.
- Option 2 throws away the strongest connection to the company and to the challenge title
  ("Future of Education"). Being different only for the sake of it is not a good reason.
- Option 3 gives something an AI cannot make up: a real story, a specific setting
  (government schools and Punjab boards), and evidence I collected myself.
  The challenge says outright that they want to see how we *identify* problems.

## Working method agreed
- Claude doesn't propose problems. It asks questions, the user answers from experience,
  and then Claude helps check each candidate (is it real, is it already solved, is it buildable).
- Every claim in the final write-up gets checked against a source or an experiment.
